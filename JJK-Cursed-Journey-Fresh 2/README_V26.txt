JJK V26 TRUE PIXEL CURSE MODELS

Hand-authored 48x64 native pixel art for six important curses: Mahito, Jogo, Hanami, Kenjaku, Dagon and Finger Bearer. Enlarged only with nearest-neighbor, formatted as the game expected 6-column x 5-row atlas. All model sheets have idle, walk, run, attack and hit poses.

Unchanged: maps, quests, coins, XP, saving, boss stats/abilities, playable character sprites and gameplay rules.
